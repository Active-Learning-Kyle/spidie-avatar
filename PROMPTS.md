# Spidie setup prompts

Copy-paste these into your own Muse's chat. Attach the named image where indicated.

## 1. Set the avatar

> *(attach `image.png`)*
>
> Change your avatar to look like the attached image — a chibi Spider-Man with an oversized round head, black over-ear headphones, and a blue-and-red hoodie with hands in the pockets.

Animated variants (idle, working, connecting, …) are generated automatically by the system once the avatar is set — there's nothing extra to do.

## 2. Switch poses

> *(attach `pose-web-shoot.png`, `pose-dance.png`, or `pose-kick.png`)*
>
> Change your avatar to this pose, keeping the same character, headphones, and hoodie.

## 3. Set the name

> Your name is Spidie from now on.

Names are per-user — every person names their own Muse.
